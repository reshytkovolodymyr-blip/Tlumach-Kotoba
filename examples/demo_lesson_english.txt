Officer: Good morning. May I see your passport?
Traveler: Certainly. Here you are.
Officer: What is the purpose of your trip?
Traveler: I am visiting my family and staying for two weeks.
Officer: Do you have a return ticket?
Traveler: Yes, I do. My return flight is on Friday.
Officer: Are you carrying any food, plants, or animal products?
Traveler: No, I am not. I only have personal belongings.
Officer: How much cash are you carrying?
Traveler: I have about one thousand dollars in cash.
Officer: All right. Thank you. You may proceed.
